# DArk: Echo Sector — Game Rules

Objective:

- Survive and clear the sector by eliminating hostile bots in each room before time runs out.

Controls:

- Move: W (up), A (left), S (down), D (right).
- Pause: `Pause` button in the HUD.
- Restart: `Restart` button in the HUD restarts the game.

HUD and Score:

- **Health:** shows current player health. If it reaches 0, the game ends.
- **Score:** increases when you destroy enemies and complete rooms.
- **Time:** the timer counts down; running out ends the game.

Bot Properties Explained (common terms):

- **Health:** how many hits the bot takes before dying.
- **Speed:** movement per frame; higher speed means it closes distance faster.
- **Damage:** how much health the bot or its bullets take from you.
- **Attack Type:** projectile (bullets), or special(like explosion)

Bullets and Wall Interaction:

- Enemy bullets can bounce off room walls; learn angles and use walls to your advantage.

Room Clearing and Spawns:

- Each room initially contains one or more bots. Clearing means all bots are destroyed.

Damage and Scoring:

- Your bullets reduce enemy health; enemies' bullets reduce your health.
- Score increases when enemies die and when rooms are cleared.

Tips & Strategy:

- Identify bot types quickly: keep distance from shooters
- Use movement to dodge bouncing bullets
- Prioritize dangerous bots (snipers/shooters) first, then clear fast bots.

Custom Bot Variants (from your notes):

1. Roamer — Dark Blue

- Description: When in chase mode this bot can roam around the room instead of staying fixed. It actively pursues the player.
- Color: dark blue
- Notes: Good for creating pressure and forcing movement.

2. Pink Immune (10 health) — Pink

- Description: A tankier bot with roughly 10 health points. Slower movement, higher durability.
- Color: pink
- Notes: Prioritize or kite; takes many hits to remove.

3. Phase Bot — Light Blue

- Description: Can disappear and reappear within the same room (teleport short distance), making it harder to reliably hit.
- Color: light blue
- Notes: Watch for reappearance points and avoid standing where it will re-spawn.

4. Teleporter — Light Purple

- Description: Can teleport to empty rooms (longer-range teleport), enabling surprise attacks from other rooms.
- Color: light purple
- Notes: Use room clearing and map awareness to predict possible teleports.

5. Explosive Bot — Red

- Description: Detonates on death, dealing area damage to the player and nearby bots.
- Color: red
- Notes: Keep distance when killing these; their explosion can injure you or chain-damage others.

6. Normal Bot — Green

- Description: Standard enemy: moderate health and behavior (patrol → alert → chase → attack → death).
- Color: green
- Notes: Most common; good practice target for beginners.

Have fun! Press Play to start the mission.
